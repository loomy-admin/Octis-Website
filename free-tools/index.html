Made for the counter

The maths a customer asks for, before the bill

  1. 01

    Answer before you bill

    What's it worth, what's my old gold worth, how much for one tola: answer in the unit the customer asks in, without opening a bill.

  2. 02

    One karat rule

    Every karat follows its hallmark: 22K is 91.6% of the 24K rate, 18K 75%, 14K 58.5%, 9K 37.5%. A tola is 10 g, and GST is split the way the bill shows it.

  3. 03

    Every line on show

    Rate, metal, making and GST each on their own line, so the customer can follow how the price was reached.
