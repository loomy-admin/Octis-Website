How it's worked out

How old gold is valued

Only the gold counts. First the fine gold: the weight × the purity it tests at. Then the value: fine gold × the jeweller's old gold rate.

That rate is the jeweller's own. It's usually lower than their selling rate, because old gold has to be melted and refined before it can be used again.

Worked example

An example, step by step

A customer brings in a 22K chain that weighs 10 g. It tests at 91.6%. The jeweller's old gold rate is ₹98,000 per 10 g of fine gold (an example, not today's rate).

  1. 1 Fine gold 10 g × 91.6% 9.160 g
  2. 2 Old gold value 9.160 g at ₹98,000 per 10 g ₹89,768

Making charges and stones from the original bill aren't part of it. Only the gold is valued.
