How it's worked out

How GST works on gold jewellery

GST on gold and silver jewellery is 3%, on the metal and the making charge together. When the shop and the customer are in the same state, it's split into CGST 1.5% + SGST 1.5%. When they're in different states, it's one line, IGST 3%.

The total is the same either way. Rates shown by jewellers and on rate boards are usually before GST.

An example, step by step

  1. 1 Making 12% of ₹1,00,000 ₹12,000
  2. 2 Before GST ₹1,00,000 + ₹12,000 ₹1,12,000
