How it's worked out

How a gold price is worked out

A jewellery price has three parts. The metal: weight × the rate for its karat. The making charge: a percentage of the metal value, or a flat amount. And GST: 3% on metal and making together.

In the same state, GST shows as CGST 1.5% + SGST 1.5%. For a customer in another state, it's IGST 3%. The total is the same either way.

Worked example

An example, step by step

Say 24K gold is ₹1,00,000 per 10 g (a round number to keep the maths easy, not today's rate). You're pricing a 22K chain that weighs 10 g, with a 12% making charge, for a customer in the same state.

  1. 1 22K rate ₹1,00,000 × 91.6% ₹91,600 /10 g
  2. 2 Metal 10 g at ₹91,600 per 10 g ₹91,600
  3. 3 Making 12% of ₹91,600 ₹10,992
  4. 4 Before GST ₹91,600 + ₹10,992 ₹1,02,592
  5. 5 GST 3% CGST ₹1,539 + SGST ₹1,539 ₹3,078
  6. 6 Estimated price What the customer pays ₹1,05,670

For a customer in another state, GST is one line, IGST 3%: ₹3,078. The total stays ₹1,05,670.
