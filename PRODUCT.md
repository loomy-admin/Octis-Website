# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and cashiers of Indian jewellery showrooms. They bill during peak hours, track gold by weight and purity, and need the books to stay inside BIS, GST, KYC and PMLA rules.

## Product Purpose

Octis is a jewellery ERP and business-intelligence platform. It records sales, purchases, payments and stock, then turns those records into reports and recommendations. Success is a free demonstration for a showroom.

## Positioning

Three products, Jewellery ERP, Pawnify and Bulion, share one security, audit, access-control and GST standard. The published difference is jewellery-specific billing, weight, purity, hallmarking and reports, not a generic retail pack.

## Operating Context

A showroom counter: old-gold exchange, split payments by cash, card and UPI, WhatsApp invoices, purity filters for 22K, 24K and 18K, and a catalogue synced from tagged stock.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed from the published Octis site: dynamic billing, stock by weight and purity, real-time catalogue sync on Professional+, AI catalogue images on Enterprise, 100+ reports, BIS and HUID tags, a PAN or Aadhaar prompt above ₹2 lakh, and a PMLA flag above ₹10 lakh cash. Pawnify and Bulion have no separate published workflow. No prices, customer names, or street address are published. The ₹4,85,200 figure is the site's own counter example.
